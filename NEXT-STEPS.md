# UroGuy website: next steps

## What's in this folder
- `index.html` is the whole website in one file. You can double-click it to open it in your browser.
- `page.html` is the same site, formatted for the online preview.

## 1. Get the domain (about $10–15 a year)
1. Go to a registrar such as Cloudflare Registrar, Porkbun or Namecheap.
2. Search for **uroguy.com**. I couldn't check it from here, so it may already be taken.
3. If it's taken, good backups: **theuroguy.com**, **uroguy.health**, **askuroguy.com**, **uroguy.co**.
4. Grab the matching social handles (@uroguy on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) at the same time, so everything matches.

## 2. Put the site online (free)
Easiest option, no coding: **Netlify Drop**
1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and make a free account.
2. Drag the `uroguy-site` folder onto the page. The site is live in seconds on a free netlify.app address.
3. In Netlify, open *Domain settings* and add your domain. Netlify shows the two settings to paste into your registrar.

(Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages also work and are free.)
